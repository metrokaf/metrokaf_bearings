About Metro Bearing International

A Dependable Partner in Industrial & Mechanical Bearing Supply

Operating from New Delhi’s primary industrial hardware hub at Shardhanand Marg (behind G.B. Road), Metro Bearing International is an established supplier of precision rolling bearings, mounted housing units, linear motion components, and linkage assemblies under our flagship brands MBI GOLD and KAF.

Industrial downtime carries a high operational cost. Every bearing series in our inventory—from G10 chrome-steel ball inserts and heavy-duty cast-iron pillow blocks to hardened linear shafts and triple-lip agricultural seals—is selected for dimensional accuracy, load endurance, and consistent rotational performance under continuous duty cycles.

Whether fulfilling an urgent single-piece replacement for a plant maintenance engineer or coordinating scheduled bulk dispatches for OEM machinery manufacturers and regional distributors, we prioritize transparent technical guidance, ready stock availability, and long-term commercial partnerships.

Chrome-Steel Metallurgy & G10 Precision

Manufactured with high-grade chrome steel raceways and G10 precision balls for low friction, reduced vibration, and extended service intervals.

Single-Unit to Bulk Wholesale Fulfillment

Structured stock depth across 180+ standard metric and inch-bore models to support both immediate retail counter pickups and volume trade orders.

Multi-Sector Application Coverage

Engineered for conveyors, CNC automation, textile and packaging lines, agricultural harvesters, HVAC blowers, and material-handling equipment.
